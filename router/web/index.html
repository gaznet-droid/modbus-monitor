How this is worked out

It follows Dad’s wiring: the Shed, Garage and 275 watt panels feed the 1000 watt inverter and the Epever; the Epever and the mains charger fill the battery, which feeds the 600 watt inverter; the Garden panel feeds the Garage inverter. The house figure is the three inverter plugs. The 275 watt panels have no meter, so they are estimated as whatever the 1000 watt inverter and the Epever drew beyond what the Shed and Garage meters measured. Battery discharge is what the 600 watt inverter drew beyond what the Epever and the charger were putting in at the time. Inverters are taken as 90 percent efficient, and the rest is shown as losses, together with any measured solar nothing was drawing. Worked out in five minute steps or longer for long periods; steps without readings from the inverters, the Epever and both meters are left out. It goes back 30 days at most.

How this is calculated

Generation is estimated from power readings collected every 15 minutes. Reverse flow contributes no generation. Gaps over 30 minutes are excluded. Bars with incomplete readings show recorded generation only. Grey hatched slots mean no data, not zero generation. Days use UK time.

Four sets of panels. The 100 watt Garden panel feeds the Garage inverter; its only reading is that inverter's eWeLink plug. The 400 watt Shed and Garage panels each pass through a meter into DC, and the 275 watt set joins DC with no meter. DC feeds the 1000 watt inverter and the Epever, which charges the 24 volt battery that runs the 600 watt inverter. Total solar to house is the three inverters added together minus what the Epever is taking in. The Generation total is the Shed and Garage meters only.
