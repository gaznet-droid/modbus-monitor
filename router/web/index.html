How this is calculated

Generation is estimated from power readings collected every 15 minutes. Reverse flow contributes no generation. Gaps over 30 minutes are excluded. Bars with incomplete readings show recorded generation only. Grey hatched slots mean no data, not zero generation. Days use UK time.
